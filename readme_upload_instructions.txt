BLUEPRINT KINGDOM MINISTRIES WEBSITE - NETLIFY UPLOAD GUIDE

1. Unzip this folder.
2. Go to Netlify and log in or create a free account.
3. Click Add new site > Deploy manually.
4. Drag this entire unzipped folder into Netlify.
5. Netlify will publish the website and give you a temporary .netlify.app address.
6. In Netlify, go to Site configuration > Domain management.
7. Add your GoDaddy domain.
8. Follow Netlify DNS instructions and update your GoDaddy DNS records.
