About Michael A. Williams

Michael A. Williams is the founder of Blueprint Kingdom Ministries, a Christian teaching and counseling ministry dedicated to helping individuals, marriages, families, and church leaders understand and apply God's design for life through biblical principles.

For nearly 40 years, Michael has faithfully served in Christian ministry through teaching, discipleship, biblical counseling, leadership development, and mentoring. His passion is to make Scripture understandable and practical, equipping believers to build strong families, healthy relationships, and Christ-centered lives.

Mission Statement

To restore God’s blueprint for family, marriage, leadership, and Kingdom purpose through biblical teaching, practical resources, and transformational ministry.
